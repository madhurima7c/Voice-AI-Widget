1. Loan Servicing Platform Redesign — Oportun (2024)
Role: Product Designer II (end-to-end ownership)
What I built: Led a full redesign of a high-traffic loan servicing platform across web and mobile. The goal was to shift users toward self-serve — reducing dependency on agents while making the loan journey more intuitive and discoverable.
Outcome: Payment conversion increased by 26%. Application creation increased by 50%.
Context: Oportun serves underbanked communities in the US. Getting this UX right had real financial consequences for real people — that stakes level made the work meaningful.
