Hi, I'm Madhurima. Maddy, for short.

I'm a late 90s kid. iPad kids didn't exist yet. I wasn't exactly the "touched grass and climbed trees" type either, but I had a fun childhood. My parents would push me outside, and I'd sneak back in to make something crafty instead. I was the birthday card maker in the family. It was serious business. People would wait for them every year.

Somewhere along the way, I fell in love with art and design. My goal has stayed simple — to create in ways that feel true, and maybe inspire someone along the way.

I've always gravitated toward the creative side of things. I spent seven years learning music, picked up a couple of instruments, and kept up with painting and illustration. I've even found joy in designing my family home. In hindsight, being a designer was probably inevitable. I went to design school in India, then spent five years working as a product designer.

Most of my career was remote, which meant I moved around a fair bit — ten trips, six cities in three years. Each place made me more curious, and eventually a little restless. I wanted to be in new rooms, have new conversations, be around people building cool things. I wanted to see what I could do in a different environment.

A year at the University of Washington is that for me. A year to learn, to get better, to be around people who push me. I graduate Fall 2026 and I'm open to opportunities.

I want to make things that matter, with people who push each other to do good work. If you like what I do and believe in what I can do, let's talk.
