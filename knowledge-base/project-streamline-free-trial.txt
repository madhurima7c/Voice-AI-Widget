STREAMLINE — Free trial / sign-up growth (2024)
Domain: B2B SaaS — growth / monetization
Timeline: 2 weeks
Role: Product Designer
Team: Founder, product marketer, developers
Site: /work/free-trial

SUMMARY CARD
Streamline let anyone browse and download without signing up — great for discovery, weak for emails and backlinks. She designed a free trial: 10 credits on premium sets for web and desktop, with abuse prevention.

THE PROBLEM
Open access meant users rarely gave emails. The business needed a fair exchange: something valuable enough to sign up, without walling off the whole library overnight.

THE SOLUTION
- Floating banner for signed-out visitors on premium sets.
- Prompt to sign up when hitting premium assets; 10 free credits on premium sets (web + desktop).
- Dev team added safeguards against duplicate/abusive sign-ups (similar email patterns).
- Idea sparked by a user on a research call: “let people test before committing.”

OUTCOMES (portfolio)
- Sign-ups ↑ 40%
- ~19K emails collected
- Upgrades ↑ 8%

Q&A
Q: Problem?
A: Too much free access without capturing emails or demonstrating premium value.

Q: Solution?
A: Limited free credits on premium content with clear signup prompts and anti-abuse checks.

Q: Domain?
A: B2B SaaS — growth and conversion design.

Q: What was her role?
A: Product designer — two-week sprint with founder, product marketer, and dev; designed trial flow and anti-abuse safeguards with engineering.

Q: What was the impact?
A: Sign-ups up 40%, ~19K emails collected, upgrades up 8%.
