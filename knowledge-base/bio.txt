Hi, I'm Madhurima. Maddy, for short.

Art and design have always brought me a lot of joy. My goal is to keep creating in ways that inspire me — and hopefully inspire people around me along the way.

I'm a late 90s kid. I wasn't exactly the "touched grass and climbed trees" type — I'd sneak back inside to make something crafty. I was the birthday card maker in the family. Serious business. People waited for them every year.

I spent seven years learning music, picked up a couple of instruments, and kept painting and illustration. Design school in India, then five years as a product designer — mostly remote, which meant moving around: ten trips, six cities in three years. Each place made me more curious.

I wanted new rooms and new conversations, so I came to Seattle for a Master's in Human-Computer Interaction + Design at the University of Washington (MHCID). I graduate Fall 2026.

As a designer, I get energy from visual design, interactions, and storytelling. Good designers should be adaptable and comfortable wearing many hats. Lately I've been exploring AI in the design process, personalization in software, and digital experiences that feel warmer and more life-like.

Outside design: films, discovering new music, concerts, and collecting inspiration from beautiful things — playlists and movie lists on my About page.
