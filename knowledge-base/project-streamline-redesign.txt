STREAMLINE — Web app redesign (2023)
Domain: B2B SaaS — design asset library (icons & illustrations)
Timeline: 4 months
Role: Product Designer
Team: Founder, growth marketer, developers
Site: /work/streamline-redesign

SUMMARY CARD
Streamline had strong assets but weak retention (~5%) and conversion (~0.9%). Madhurima led a full app redesign: research, layout exploration, three-column “browse / view / customize” model, design system, and staged launch.

THE PROBLEM
Users signed up but didn't upgrade or stick around. Mixpanel showed funnel drop-off and low feature usage; interviews revealed overwhelm (e.g. two confusing color editors), poor findability, and workflows that didn't match how designers actually use asset libraries.

THE SOLUTION
- Mixed Mixpanel funnel data with light-user interviews and power-user research.
- Explored four layout directions; chose three-column layout (find → view → customize) like pro tools users already know.
- Simplified workflows, improved perceived value, designed for future feature growth without bloat.
- Testing at 5% of light users; fixed export-format discoverability (formats were hidden in a dropdown — surfaced in panel; export usage jumped).
- Shipped redesign at streamlinehq.com.

OUTCOMES (portfolio)
- Feature discovery ↑ 46%
- User retention ↑ 13%
- Conversion ↑ ~2%
