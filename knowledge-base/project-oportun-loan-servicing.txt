OPORTUN — Loan servicing platform redesign (2025)
Domain: B2C fintech (online loan servicing — web + mobile)
Timeline: 2 months
Role: Product Designer (sole designer for online servicing)
Team: Design manager, PMs, developers
Site: /work/loan-servicing

SUMMARY CARD
Oportun serves ~2.2M members with limited credit history. ~800K active loan members were calling support ~116K times/month because the app didn't answer basic payment, balance, and offer questions. Madhurima mapped loan states and redesigned payment + loan-detail flows with a “transparency forward” approach.

THE PROBLEM
Support costs and delinquency were rising. Self-serve existed on web and mobile, but members still called when they couldn't find what they owed, whether a payment posted, or hardship/renewal eligibility. Mobile web was where most servicing logins happened, but design and the JS stack lagged behind the native app.

THE SOLUTION
- Contextual payment widget on home: one tap to pay, amount consistent across states.
- Loan details: glanceable numbers first, progressive disclosure, jargon explained, real-time updates when payments post or fail.
- Receipts in-app and for download mirror payment confirmation structure (amount, allocation, balance).
- Mapped loan states (current, past due, hardship, paid ahead, paid off, etc.) across home / summary / extended views.
- Moved renewal and hardship offers out of a buried carousel into fixed top-of-fold slots.
- Added reusable components to Storybook; staged rollout to 15% then wider launch.

DESIGN SYSTEMS (member app + Zeus)
- **Existing system (member-facing):** Extended and updated components for the loan servicing experience — payment widget, loan detail patterns, receipts — documented in Storybook for consistent dev handoff.
- **New system (Zeus):** Built a full design system from scratch for Zeus, Oportun's agent-facing internal tool — 200+ components, styles, and patterns so agent workflows stayed consistent and scalable.

OUTCOMES (1 month post launch, portfolio figures)
- Feature discoverability ↑ 395%
- App logins ↑ 64%
- Customer calls ↓ 29%
- Portfolio also notes Oportun won “America's Best Customer Service in Financial Services” (2026).
