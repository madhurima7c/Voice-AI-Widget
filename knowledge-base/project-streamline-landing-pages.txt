STREAMLINE — Custom landing pages (2024)
Domain: B2B SaaS — marketing / growth (Framer)
Timeline: 2 months
Role: Product Designer
Team: Founder
Site: /work/landing-page

SUMMARY CARD
Visitors didn't know which icon set to pick or how to use Streamline in real projects. Support tickets and research showed demand for use-case examples, not just a catalog. She designed use-case-driven landing pages and turned them into Framer CMS templates.

THE PROBLEM
Users felt unsure Streamline fit their project. They wanted practical examples (interfaces, presentations, signage, etc.), not only a flat catalog of sets.

THE SOLUTION
- Started with one page per icon set — not scalable as the library grew.
- Landed on one template per use case: hero, recommended sets, visual examples (interfaces, graphic design, product design, presentations, diagrams, signage).
- Built first pages in Framer, then CMS templates for consistency and speed.
- Created sample use-case visuals using Streamline sets themselves.

OUTCOMES
- Higher website engagement; users referenced use cases inside the product.
- Fewer “how do I use this?” support queries; team could link to landing sections.
