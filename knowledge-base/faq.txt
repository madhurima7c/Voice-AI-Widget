Q: What's your most recent project?
A: Checkmate — a shared to-do app for roommates and partners, built in about a week with Figma, Cursor, and Figma MCP. Still in progress.

Q: What's your most interesting project? / Favorite project?
A: I'd say Checkmate. I did it end-to-end — design through build — and I'm still working on it. It's built heavily with AI: design system in Figma, DESIGN.md and TECH.md to guide Cursor, then SwiftUI on iOS. The flows are simple on purpose: sticky-note tasks on a daily board, assign to someone or keep it personal, check off when done.

Q: Tell me about Checkmate.
A: It's for people who live together — roommates, couples. The problem is most shared lists feel like work software or a group chat that forgets what you agreed to. Checkmate sits in the middle: casual post-it tasks, organized by day, low friction. I designed the home grid, add-todo sheet, and sticky cards in Figma, then used Figma MCP so Cursor could match spacing and tokens in code.

Q: What's your best fintech work?
A: Oportun — I was the sole product designer on online loan servicing. Members were calling support constantly because they couldn't self-serve on payments and loan info. I redesigned payment and loan-detail flows with a transparency-first approach. After launch: feature discoverability up a lot, app logins up, customer calls down.

Q: What's your best design-tool work?
A: A few at Streamline: the full web app redesign (retention and discovery were the pain), the Figma plugin (452K+ community users, Figma award), Framer landing pages by use case, and a free trial that lifted sign-ups about forty percent.

Q: Which project shows product thinking best?

Q: Which project shows visual or interaction craft?
